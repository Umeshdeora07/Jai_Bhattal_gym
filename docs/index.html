FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to buy supplements or protein powder?

No. Every plan meets your protein and nutrition needs through whole, home-made food. That is the heart of the method.

I'm vegetarian. Will this work for me?

Yes. Plans are built around what you already eat, whether you are vegetarian, eggetarian or non-vegetarian.

Do you use medicines, injections or fat burners?

No. The approach is completely natural: no medicines, no weight-loss injections and no fat burners.

Is coaching only for people in India?

No. Coaching is fully online, and clients join from abroad too. Your plan uses the foods available where you live.

I have a medical condition. Can I still join?

Tell Jai about it when you first message. Jai is trained in exercise programming for conditions such as thyroid disease, and your plan will work alongside your doctor's advice, never replace it.

Which plan should I choose?

3 months builds the base. 6 months gives time for a visible change. 9 months covers the full journey and is the best value per month. Not sure? Ask Jai on WhatsApp.
